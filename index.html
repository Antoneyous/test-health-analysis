Task / Difficulty
Avg. Score ?The average score candidates received on this task. Very high (above 85%) usually means the task is too easy. Very low (under 15%) usually means it is too hard or broken.
Discrimination ?How well this task separates stronger candidates from weaker ones. Values at or above 0.75 are strong. Values close to zero mean the task is not adding much signal. Negative values mean weaker candidates tended to score higher on this task than stronger ones, which usually points to a problem with the task itself.
N ?Number of candidates who attempted this task.
Weight ?How much this task slot contributes to a candidate's overall score.
